The schema dictionary `provenance/csv_schema.json` enumerates each CSV's columns and row count. In public-data result files, `frame_key` is a timestamp-derived anchor identifier, `tp/fp/fn` are integer counts, `f1/precision/recall` are fractions, `matched_mean_iou` is conditional on matched predictions, and `scope` determines the GT population. Prediction geometry uses metres and yaw radians. In simulation files, `timestamp` is the recorded simulation time; Box2D evaluation ignores yaw as required by that archived protocol. Do not merge these evaluator families.
